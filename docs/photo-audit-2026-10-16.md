# Website image audit — October 16, 2026

The owner rejected the old Nags Head panorama and requested attractive homes and town imagery throughout the site. Reviewed all 54 canonical pages and all 19 unique guide photographs, including the Duck presentation override. Replaced 17 weak/redundant photographs with a curated 14-photo library; retained the approved Duck sunset and Cape Hatteras Lighthouse photograph. The homepage painting, Piper branding, Allen illustration and decorative image treatment remain intact.

## Changes

- Removed roadside traffic, highway snapshots, the welcome sign and the rejected Nags Head panorama.
- Selected coastal homes, village views, waterfront architecture and local landmarks with verified sources and licenses.
- Self-hosted 640/1280 WebP versions, explicit dimensions, responsive sizes and reviewed focal positions.
- Added accurate descriptive alt text; synchronized visible images with Article schema, Open Graph and Twitter previews.
- Preserved exact titles, descriptions, H1-H3, sitemap, routes, non-photo links, body copy, policies, GA4, form handling and email automation.
- Regional substitutions are clearly captioned with their actual location; they are not presented as photographs of the requested town. A commissioned local photo set can replace those later without changing page structure.
