# Memory

## Project
OBXNCInsurance.com is a public-facing education and lead-prep website for Outer Banks, NC homeowners, second-home owners, vacation-rental owners, buyers, and community members.

## Preferences
- Website copy must always speak to real homeowners and community members. Never use internal SEO, targeting, ranking, query, or strategy language in visitor-facing content.
- Do not write phrases like "this page targets", "this query", "Google is testing", "Search Console shows", "rank for", or "searches should land" on the website. Those are internal instructions only.
- Keep copy transparent, educated, informed, kind, local, and human. Explain what owners need to know and what to prepare.
