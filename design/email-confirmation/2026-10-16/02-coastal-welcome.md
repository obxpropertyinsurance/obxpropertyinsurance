# A coastal welcome

An artful, premium confirmation that feels like a welcome card from the Outer Banks. The approved coastal painting and warm Piper palette carry the website’s personality into the inbox. The receipt stays above the image; the email choice and requested date stay in real, readable text.

**Subject:** Jamie, we have your home insurance request

**Alternate subject:** Your OBX insurance request is in, Jamie

**Preheader:** We have your request, your timing, and your preference for email.

**Goal:** Reassure a buyer approaching closing that the request arrived, their preferences were heard, and a human review comes next. An optional reply gives them a simple way to update the request without restarting the funnel.

**Primary action:** “Reply with an update” → hello@obxncinsurance.com. No reply is required and the email says so explicitly.

**Personalization:** First name, buying-home request context, exact requested coverage date, and email contact preference. All example content is fictional. This version is for eligible email-first submissions only. Other channels, dates, missing data, and holds need their own truthful conditional copy.

**Human versus Piper:** Piper appears as the friendly bird guide at the end. A separate heading explains human review; the bird never signs as a real person or licensed advisor.

**Design choices:** 600px table structure; restrained full-width painting; generous cream space; navy Fraunces typography with a Georgia fallback; coral reply button. The visual character is strong without borrowing common insurance icons or newsletter grids. No all-cap labels, tracking pixels, customer property address, or phone number.

**Tradeoffs:** More memorable and warm than the letter, but a longer message and an extra image load. Readers still get a full confirmation when images or the custom font are blocked. The compact recap borrows the structured option’s practical clarity. The personal letter is likely the fastest to scan; this direction offers the closest continuation of the approved website identity.

**Review-only implementation:** This HTML is a browser-ready design concept with relative links to existing, approved assets. Before production, replace image paths with verified public HTTPS URLs, optimize the painting and Piper assets for email delivery, create a plain-text equivalent, and test Gmail/Outlook/Apple Mail and image-blocked rendering. Desktop and mobile browser previews are not evidence of email-client compatibility. No email was sent and no live template was changed.
